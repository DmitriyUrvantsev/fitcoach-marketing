Connected coaching for trainers and clients

One coaching system.
Two connected apps.

Fitness Coach Client Tracker connects trainers and their clients through dedicated apps built to work together in real time.

Trainers create and send programs. Clients follow them, record their results, and send their training, nutrition, calorie, and progress data back to the trainer.

How it works

From the trainer's plan to the client's results.

The trainer and client use separate apps, but they work as one connected coaching system.

Real-time coaching

Fitness Coach Client Tracker brings this information together between the trainer and client apps, so both sides work with current data.

The result is a more connected coaching workflow from program creation to execution and progress tracking.
